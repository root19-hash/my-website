Our Printing Services

Mathason Library Printing Services offers a range of printing and photocopying options to help students, staff, and library users with their academic and personal printing needs.

Black and White Printing

Affordable black and white printing for assignments, notes, reports, study materials, and other documents.

Colour Printing

High-quality colour printing for presentations, posters, projects, and documents that need a professional appearance.

Photocopying

Photocopying services are available for study materials, documents, notes, and other suitable materials.

Available Paper Sizes

Our printing service provides the following paper sizes:

  • A4
  • A3

Printing Types

Users can select from the following printing options:

  • Single-sided printing
  • Double-sided printing
  • Black and white printing
  • Colour printing

How to Use the Printing Service

  1. Prepare the document you want to print.
  2. Check the printing service, paper size, and print type you need.
  3. Complete the Print Request Form on our Contact & Booking page.
  4. Submit your printing request.
  5. Contact the library if you need assistance with your request.
